# Research, provenance and publication policy

This is a project policy and general legal context, not legal clearance or individual legal advice. Freeware and discontinued software remain subject to applicable rights and terms. Open-source licensing Angle does not license Arc. No contributor location disclosure is required; applicable law and the terms governing each person's copy still matter.

Arc's published terms prohibit reverse engineering and account sharing. Statutory exceptions depend on jurisdiction and purpose. US 17 USC 1201(f) is limited to qualifying interoperability activity, not a general cloning exemption. EU Directive 2009/24/EC distinguishes normal observation from narrowly conditioned decompilation. Independently written code does not automatically clear expressive UI copying, trademark, patent, contract or access-control questions. Obtain qualified advice or permission when the legal basis is uncertain; switch to ordinary documented behavior observation while that question is unresolved.

## Permitted contribution paths

1. Normal-use observation by someone entitled to use their Arc installation, using their own locally controlled account and synthetic test data. Record OS, build, date, starting state, exact actions, visible/persistent results and uncertainty.
2. Bounded static RE where the contributor has assessed lawful access, applicable terms and a basis for the specific activity. The issue must state the interoperability or research question, minimal target range, time budget and expected factual deliverable. Maintainer approval is project scope review, not a legal opinion.
3. Independent implementation against reviewed behavior/interface specifications. Do not translate pseudocode or reconstruct proprietary functions into Angle source.

No authentication bypass, account transfer, signature patching, service impersonation, remote probing, leaked source, or redistribution of proprietary code/assets is included in this project. Review new research methods separately; a public issue does not authorize access to anyone else's systems.

## Evidence handling

Keep original installers, binaries, decompiler output, assembly, raw compiler-metadata byte strings, personal profiles, screenshots and recordings under ignored local artifacts/. Do not send these to external AI services without rights and privacy assessment. Public reports contain original descriptions, necessary factual names, hashes, offsets, tool versions, reproduction commands and limitations. Hashes identify artifacts; they do not grant redistribution rights.

The initial publication removes byte/disassembly excerpts from earlier JSON reports; original local reports are retained under artifacts/prepublication-evidence/. Reports are publication summaries and may differ from the raw tool output reproduced locally. Metadata names and addresses are findings, not a recovered implementation or verified disk schema. This is not a claim of strict clean-room development: earlier contributors and agents inspected binaries. Declare relevant exposure and sources rather than asserting a separation that did not happen.

Angle has independent branding and no affiliation with The Browser Company or Google's ANGLE project. Functional fidelity is a goal, not permission to copy protected expression. Keep MPL-2.0 and dependency obligations separate; binary distribution requires a versioned license/attribution review.
